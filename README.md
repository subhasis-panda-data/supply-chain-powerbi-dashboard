supply-chain-powerbi-dashboard

### 🔍 Problem Statement

Supply chain operations face several challenges that impact delivery performance, inventory management, and operational efficiency:

- **Delayed Deliveries:** Orders fail to reach customers within the expected timelines.
- **Partial Deliveries:** Customers receive less than the full quantity ordered.
- **Inventory Shortages:** Inadequate stock planning can lead to product unavailability and fulfillment issues.
- **Transport Inefficiency:** Inefficient transportation planning can increase delivery delays and operational costs.

My Key Power BI Contributions:

KPI Development: Created KPI cards to track Total Orders, On-Time Delivery (OT%), In-Full Delivery (IF%), and OTIF%.

Regional Analysis: Built bar charts to compare OTIF%, OT%, and IF% across regions.

Trend Analysis: Developed a monthly OTIF trend chart to monitor delivery performance over time.

Delay Analysis: Visualized average delivery delays by warehouse location and transport mode.

Quantity Comparison: Created comparative charts for ordered vs. delivered quantities across months.

Interactive Reporting: Added slicers for warehouse location, transport mode, region, and month to enable dynamic analysis.

### 💡 Data-Driven Recommendations Based on Dashboard Analysis

Using Power BI dashboards and KPI analysis, I identified opportunities to improve supply chain visibility and support better business decisions:

1. **Improve Warehouse Stock Planning:** Monitor inventory levels and align stock availability with demand.
2. **Optimize Transport Routing:** Analyze transport performance and delays to improve delivery efficiency.
3. **Focus on Underperforming Regions:** Compare regional OTIF%, on-time delivery, and in-full delivery KPIs to identify performance gaps.
4. **Strengthen Supplier Coordination:** Improve communication and delivery planning to support timely and complete order fulfillment.

### 🎯 Business Objective

Leverage data visualization and KPI monitoring to identify performance gaps, improve delivery reliability, optimize logistics, and enable data-driven supply chain decisions.
